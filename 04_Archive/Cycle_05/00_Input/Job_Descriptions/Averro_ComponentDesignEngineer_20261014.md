About the job
Title: Component Design Engineer

Location: Fully Remote within the US

Pay Range: $52-$95/hr DOE & Location

*Compensation varies based on skills, experience, and location where candidate lives.

Benefits: Medical, Dental, and Vision insurance, 401k, sick leave, and employee assistance program. Depending on your package selection, Averro offers PTO, paid holidays, and legal insurance.

Employment Type: Long-term contract with option for unlimited extensions and conversion to direct hire



Our advanced nuclear client is seeking Component Design Engineers to support the design and development of reactor structures and mechanical equipment for an advanced Generation IV nuclear reactor program. These roles are focused on the Rotatable Plug Assembly, a major mechanical system within the reactor enclosure.



The team is looking for engineers with strong equipment/component design experience who enjoy solving complex mechanical problems and working closely with analysis, manufacturing, and supplier teams throughout the design lifecycle.



What you’ll work on:

Design reactor structures and mechanical components from requirements through final design
Develop equipment and component design solutions in coordination with engineering and project teams
Perform scoping calculations and support mechanical/stress analysis activities
Participate in and support design reviews
Assess technical solutions and collaborate with engineering teams on design improvements
Support fabrication and supplier issue resolution during design and manufacturing
Work closely with multidisciplinary engineering teams on integration and design execution
Apply nuclear industry engineering standards and best practices throughout the design process


What they are looking for:

BS in Mechanical Engineering or related field
4+ years or 8+ years (Senior Engineer) of mechanical equipment/component design experience
Experience with ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code Section III
Experience writing engineering/design specifications
Experience with Design for Manufacturability and Design for Assembly
Experience with finite element stress analysis and mechanical analysis methods
Experience using ANSYS or similar mechanical stress analysis tools
CAD experience required; SolidWorks preferred
Strong communication, technical problem-solving, and teamwork skills
Ability to work independently and manage multiple technical tasks


Preferred experience:

Nuclear industry or other highly regulated engineering environments
Large mechanical assemblies or rotating equipment
Slewing ring bearings
Elastomeric seals


This is a good opportunity for engineers who enjoy hands-on mechanical design work, complex equipment development, and collaborating with highly technical teams supporting next-generation reactor technology.

## Final Top 3 Selections

### 1. PhysicsX – Research Scientist (AI-Driven Multi-Physics Simulation)
**Final Score in Matrix:** 68  
**Rationale for Selection:**
- Highest overall match score (70) with strong technical alignment in multi-physics simulation, surrogate modeling, and Python scientific computing.
- Semiconductor customer base directly relevant to candidate's microelectronics/PhD/ASML background.
- Excellent pay range ($120K–$240K) + equity + comprehensive benefits.
- "Work from anywhere" flexibility provides a pathway to remote work despite hybrid NY base.
- Publication record and research-to-production experience align with role requirements.

**Why Selected Over Others:** Best balance of technical fit, compensation, and remote flexibility among the top 4.

---

### 2. Foundation EGI – Research Scientist (AI Copilot for Design/Manufacturing)
**Final Score in Matrix:** 65  
**Rationale for Selection:**
- Remote-first environment is the strongest location fit, satisfying the fully remote preference.
- Strong Python, algorithm development, and LLM post-training alignment with candidate's recent workflow integrations.
- Dataset curation and simulation work overlap with surrogate modeling pipelines at LANL.
- Full-time role with benefits (medical/dental/vision, retirement savings).

**Why Selected:** Only role in the top 4 that is genuinely remote-first. Pay verification is needed, but location alignment is a hard constraint win.

### 3. Microsoft – Data Research Engineer (Multimodal AI / Superintelligence Team)
**Final Score in Matrix:** 62  
**Rationale for Selection:**
- ABQ (Albuquerque) hybrid arrangement is acceptable per human clarification — Microsoft has a presence in the Albuquerque area, making 4 days/week office requirement feasible without relocation.
- Role involves large-scale data processing, curation, pipeline development, and statistical analysis — transferable skills from surrogate modeling dataset work and Python scientific computing at LANL.
- Outstanding compensation ($119.8K–$274.8K, higher in certain metros) and world-class Microsoft benefits.
- Prestigious company brand and career growth potential within Microsoft AI.
- 4+ years (IC4) or 8+ years (IC5) requirement easily exceeded by PhD + 7+ years R&D experience.

**Why Selected (Elevated):** Human override based on ABQ hybrid acceptability and data processing fit. This moves Microsoft ahead of NVIDIA (location blocker) and Solid Power (manually downgraded).

**Gap Note:** The role has a data annotation/curations flavor (deprioritized in original preferences). However, the data pipeline and large-scale dataset work components are a reasonable fit, and the ABQ hybrid makes it viable.

### Job 001: Lightbridge - Principal/Senior Mechanical Engineer (Fuel Assembly Design)

**Match Score:** 84/100 (Rank #1)

**Why Selected:**
- Highest overall match score with exceptional nuclear domain alignment
- Remote work culture meets top location preference
- Strong FEA (ABAQUS/ANSYS) and CAD experience directly transferable
- Nuclear fuel performance modeling background (LANL MOOSE/BISON) is highly relevant
- Comprehensive benefits package (100% health coverage, 401k match)

**Key Strengths as Candidate:**
- Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering meets education requirement
- Extensive FEA/FEM experience with implicit/explicit codes
- Thermal-mechanical simulation under extreme conditions (high temperature, neutron fluence)
- Technical leadership and cross-functional collaboration documented at LANL
- R&D 100 Award Finalist recognition demonstrates innovation capability

**Addressable Gaps:**
- Nuclear fuel assembly design specifics: Learnable through study; fundamentals of structural analysis are strong
- DEFORM/Autodesk tools: Not documented but FEA concepts are transferable
- Nuclear licensing submittals: Not directly documented; can be addressed through willingness to learn

**Personal Interest & Motivation:**
High - Direct application of nuclear materials and mechanics expertise to advanced nuclear fuel design. Represents strategic alignment with clean energy / advanced reactor technology sector.

**Decision Rationale:**
Selected as #1 due to highest match score, fully remote work arrangement, and strongest transferability of FEA and nuclear materials experience. Lightbridge's focus on metallic nuclear fuel aligns with candidate's HT-9/Grade 91 steel modeling background.

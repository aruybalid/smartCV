About the job
Looking to lead physics simulation for a firm who are in pole position to dominate a $100 trillion global home market?



A well-funded, venture backed insurtech who are shortly set to make profit after just two years of operation, is hiring a Physics Simulation Team Lead as they expand across the country and into different risk areas. Comfort with Fortran-based solvers, Python/C++, HPC workflows, and people leadership is required. Experience with surrogate modeling, ML-physics integration, or physics based risk-modeling of natural hazards is a strong plus.

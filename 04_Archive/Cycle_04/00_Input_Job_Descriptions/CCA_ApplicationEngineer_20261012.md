About the job
Mechanical Application Engineer

Base Salary: $110K–$120K

Primary Location: Remote

Travel: 15%



 SUMMARY:

 We’re hiring a Mechanical Application Engineer to support the design and development of

 offsite manufactured and modular building components for complex projects.

 This role requires a strong mechanical systems background, with specific experience in P&ID

 diagramming, hydronic piping, HVAC systems, chillers, heating systems, and related mechanical

 infrastructure. The ideal candidate will likely come from an HVAC mechanical contractor, a

 facility engineering role within a university, or a facility engineering role within a hospital or

 healthcare system.



This is a hands-on engineering role for someone who can combine technical depth with practical

execution. You’ll help design mechanical systems for modular and offsite construction

applications while ensuring each solution meets performance, safety, compliance, transport,

manufacturing, and installation requirements.



What you’ll do

Develop mechanical system designs for offsite manufacturing products and systems across multiple complex projects
Create and interpret P&ID diagrams for piping, instrumentation, HVAC, chiller, and heating systems
Design and support hydronic piping systems, especially those tied to HVAC, chilled water, and heating applications
Collaborate with design, mechanical, electrical, structural, and construction teams to ensure integrated project execution
Create detailed engineering drawings, bills of material, and technical models or simulations
Participate in client-facing and internal coordination discussions to solve design challenges, reduce risk, and finalize project solutions
Make sound engineering decisions in the review and approval of drawings, specifications, and project documentation
Review vendor drawings and confirm alignment with engineering requirements
Evaluate materials and construction methods for performance, constructability, and cost efficiency
Partner with manufacturing teams to support designs that improve production, transportation, assembly, and field installation
Conduct shop and field visits to verify constructability and support operational success
Stay current on evolving engineering methods and innovations related to offsite and modular construction
Contribute to research and development efforts tied to new product development and continuous improvement
Prepare technical studies, specifications, cost estimates, and proposal evaluations
 

What we’re looking for

 Bachelor’s degree in Engineering from an ABET-accredited institution
 4+ years of engineering analysis and design experience within mechanical building systems, HVAC, hydronic piping, chiller systems, heating systems, or related infrastructure
Required experience with P&ID diagramming, including piping and instrumentation design for mechanical systems
Required experience with hydronic piping systems, preferably within HVAC, chilled water, heating, or central plant environments
Background working for an HVAC mechanical contractor, or as a Facility Engineer within a university, hospital, healthcare system, or similar large-campus environment
Experience with chillers, heating systems, mechanical rooms, central utility plants, or complex facility mechanical systems strongly preferred
PE license preferred
Strong communication skills with the ability to work across teams and interact effectively with clients
Ability to explain technical concepts clearly to non-technical audiences
Strong technical writing and documentation skills
Familiarity with applicable building and engineering codes such as ASCE, AISC, and IBC
Ability to produce and manage engineering drawings independently and in coordination with broader design teams
Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple project demands at once Sound judgment in engineering decisions and project execution
Ability to interpret contract documents, drawings, specifications, and scopes of work Proactive, solutions-oriented mindset
Ability to manage deadlines and competing priorities in a fast-moving environment
Experience with engineering and design tools such as SAP2000, STAAD, Revit, Inventor, TEKLA, AutoCAD, or similar platforms
Strong computer skills, including Microsoft Office and intermediate Excel capability
 

Why consider this opportunity

 This is a strong opportunity for a mechanical engineer who wants to work on complex, real-

 world projects in the growing offsite and modular construction space. You’ll have the chance to

 influence mechanical design quality, improve manufacturability, and help bring high-

 performance building solutions from concept through production and field execution.

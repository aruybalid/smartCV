About the Role

The Nuclear Design Engineer (Junior or Senior) supports the core design and neutronics methodology development and analysis of Lightbridge Fuel for various water‑cooled reactor applications. This role contributes to fuel design, analysis, licensing qualification, and commercialization through neutronics modeling, technical documentation, and collaboration with multidisciplinary teams. The scope of responsibility, technical ownership, and leadership increases with experience level.

Key Responsibilities

Perform neutronics and core design analyses in support of the licensing qualification and commercialization of Lightbridge Fuel for water‑cooled reactors.
Develop, document, and maintain lattice physics and full‑core neutronics models using deterministic and Monte Carlo analysis tools.
Integrate neutronics results with thermal‑hydraulics and fuel performance analyses.
Develop and utilize software tools and workflows, including Python‑based scripting, to automate analyses, post‑process results, and improve efficiency.
Explore or apply AI and data‑driven techniques (e.g., machine learning, surrogate models, automated optimization) to enhance fuel design, modeling efficiency, or decision‑making.
Prepare technical calculations, reports, and licensing support documentation in compliance with quality and regulatory requirements.
Collaborate with internal project teams, leadership, and stakeholders to meet technical, schedule, and quality objectives.
Senior level: Lead complex analyses, resolve advanced technical challenges, review and approve technical work products, and interface with regulatory agencies, utilities, vendors, and partners.
Senior level: Mentor and provide technical guidance to junior engineers and support engineering best practices.

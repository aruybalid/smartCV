We are looking for an experienced image processing algorithm developer to join the Applied Material in Santa Clara. In this position you will work with process engineers, and algorithm developers to develop and deploy custom image processing algorithms, to be used for accelerating semiconductor process development. You will be exposed to the wide portfolio of technologies and products developed in Applied Materials and will work directly with the algorithm users for continuous feedback and improvement as part of the semiconductor process development.

Requires specialized depth and/or breadth of expertise. Interprets internal or external business issues and recommends best practices. Solves complex problems; takes a broad perspective to identify innovative solutions. Works independently, with guidance in only the most complex situations. May lead functional teams or projects.

Key Responsibilities

Develop and implement image processing and Machine Learning algorithms
Work closely with application engineers to generate optimal images
Demonstrate and communicate algorithm performance to internal and external users
Collaborate with internal and external groups to generate data, validate results, and deploy algorithms

Skills And Experience

Fluent in Python
Proven record of image processing, Machine Learning and computer vision algorithm development
3+ years hand-on algorithm development experience
Experience with Open CV, PyTorch/Tensorflow
Experience with optimizing computational efficiency preferred
Experience with electron beam image processing preferred
Experience with semiconductor metrology and inspection preferred

Education

M.Sc. or Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Physics, or related fields.

Additional Skills

Clear and effective communication
Willing to work with groups in different time zones
Able to handle and prioritize multiple simultaneous projects

Overview

We are seeking a Physicist/Scientist to develop and maintain firmware and electronics for high‑performance hardware control framework to support advanced imaging platforms. This role sits at the intersection of firmware design, electrical design and system integration. The ideal candidate must be familiar with the architecture of imaging systems and its key components, be comfortable working hands-on in the lab building benchtop prototypes and contribute to long‑term system architecture and reliability. Close collaboration with hardware and external software teams is also required.

Role Responsibilities: 

Build and test benchtop imaging prototypes. Develop and maintain a framework for hardware control, including motion stages, cameras, lasers, detectors, and other imaging components. Design and implement workflows for automated experiments. 
Read, understand, and upgrade our existing software framework, extending functionality while maintaining backward compatibility and code quality. 
Write robust, efficient C/C++ code for hardware-interaction and performance-critical modules. 
Design control logics for subsystems or added devices and integrate them into the main system. 
Design efficient data handling strategies. 
Debug and troubleshoot complex hardware–software issues using lab instruments. 
Collaborate closely with the hardware engineers to deliver integrated system solutions. 
Design simple electrical circuits to support high‑speed data acquisition and system control. 
Develop and maintain documentation, including software architecture diagrams, interface definitions, and testing frameworks to ensure long‑term code reliability and maintainability. 
Collaborate with external software teams to define and implement interfaces between control backends and higher‑level software systems. 

Minimum Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree or higher in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Physics, Biomedical Engineering, or related disciplines. 
Strong proficiency of modern C/C++ and Python for hardware control. 
5+ years of hands-on experience with lab instrumentation and the ability to integrate diverse devices by developing custom control and automation framework. 
Demonstrated ability to read, understand, maintain, and enhance large, existing codebases. 
Ability to read and understand schematics and basic circuit designs. 
Solid understanding of real‑time constraints and low‑level hardware interfaces. 

Preferred Qualifications: 

Familiarity with camera SDKs and image acquisition workflows. 
Hands-on experience with optical systems design and alignment. 
Experience maintaining or refactoring large-scale scientific or industrial control software systems. 
Familiarity with high‑speed data acquisition, imaging pipelines, or precision instrumentation. 
Experience developing automated tests or experiment frameworks. 
Knowledge of version control, code reviews, and software quality best practices.

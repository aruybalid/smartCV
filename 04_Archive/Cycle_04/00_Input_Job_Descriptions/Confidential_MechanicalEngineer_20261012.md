The Role

We are looking for a talented Mechanical Engineer to join our distributed hardware engineering team. Whether you are a mid-career engineer looking to broaden your scope in consumer electronics/industrial automation or a senior-level Mechanical Architect ready to lead critical structural and thermal system decisions, we have a spot for you on our ladder. You will work across the full product development lifecycle, bridging the gap between mechanical design, manufacturing, and cross-functional engineering teams to ensure product reliability, performance, and manufacturability.

What You’ll Do

Design & Develop: Design, simulate, and prototype complex mechanical systems, including enclosures, structural assemblies, thermal management solutions, and precision electromechanical components using CAD tools (SolidWorks, NX, or CATIA).
Collaborate: Work closely with Electrical Engineers, Firmware Engineers, and Product Managers in an Agile hardware environment to define mechanical requirements, form factors, tolerances, and thermal/power budgets.
Testing & Validation: Ensure high reliability and compliance; design and execute rigorous test plans (EVT/DVT/PVT), perform physical prototype bring-up, and conduct root cause analysis on mechanical failures including fatigue, stress, and thermal performance issues.
Architecture: Contribute to system-wide architectural decisions (material selection, actuation systems, structural design) and help evolve the mechanical stack to meet future product roadmaps.
DFM & Supply Chain: Work with contract manufacturers (CMs) to optimize designs for manufacturing (DFM), assembly (DFA), and test (DFT); manage component selection and BOM to mitigate supply chain risks.
Compliance & Safety: Implement rigorous design standards for thermal management, ingress protection (IP ratings), drop/vibration testing, and safety certifications (UL, CE, RoHS).
Mentorship: (For Senior+ roles) Mentor junior engineers and provide technical leadership on complex design challenges, tolerance stack-up analysis, and drawing/GD&T reviews.
Maintenance: Troubleshoot, debug, and upgrade existing mechanical platforms; manage lifecycle updates for EOL components and maintain vendor technical relationships.

What We’re Looking For

Education: Bachelor's degree or higher in Mechanical Engineering or a closely related field.
Experience: 3–10+ years of hands-on mechanical engineering experience in consumer electronics, industrial automation, robotics, or a related hardware-focused industry.
CAD Proficiency: Strong experience with 3D CAD tools such as SolidWorks, PTC Creo, Siemens NX, or CATIA; proficiency in generating detailed engineering drawings with GD&T.
Simulation & Analysis: Experience with FEA/FEM tools (ANSYS, Abaqus, or SolidWorks Simulation) for structural, thermal, and dynamic analysis.
Tolerance & GD&T: Strong command of geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T) and tolerance stack-up analysis.
Cross-Functional Communication: Excellent written and verbal communication skills; ability to collaborate effectively in a fully remote, distributed team environment.

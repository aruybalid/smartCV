## Strategic Decisions and Rationale

### 1. **Emphasize FEA Troubleshooting Over Domain**
- **Decision:** Focused on FEA workflow troubleshooting and problem-solving rather than nuclear materials domain
- **Rationale:** The role is about supporting Ansys FEA customers with technical issues, not nuclear domain expertise. Highlighting troubleshooting and problem-solving capabilities provides better alignment than nuclear materials focus.

### 2. **Highlight Customer-Facing Experience**
- **Decision:** Emphasized ASML industrial collaboration and customer support experience
- **Rationale:** The role requires "strong interpersonal and communication skills" and working "one on one with customers." The ASML experience demonstrates customer-facing capability, even though it's not Ansys-specific.

### 3. **Connect MOOSE/BISON to Ansys Support**
- **Decision:** Explicitly stated MOOSE/BISON FEA experience is "directly transferable to Ansys FEA support"
- **Rationale:** While the job requires Ansys-specific experience, the transferable FEA skills and troubleshooting approach are the core competencies. Making this connection explicit helps bridge the software gap.

### 4. **Emphasize Python Tool Development**
- **Decision:** Highlighted "custom Python-based analysis tools" and "workflow automation"
- **Rationale:** The job mentions "Basic scripting and PowerShell experience a positive." Python tool development demonstrates scripting capability and ability to automate workflows, which is valuable for support work.

### 5. **Acknowledge Support vs. Hands-On R&D**
- **Decision:** Positioned troubleshooting and support experience as strengths rather than avoiding the support role framing
- **Rationale:** The job is explicitly a support role. Embracing this positioning and demonstrating relevant support/troubleshooting experience is more authentic than trying to reframe as hands-on R&D.

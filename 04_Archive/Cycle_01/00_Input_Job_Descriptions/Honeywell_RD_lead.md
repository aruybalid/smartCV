
As a Lead Research and Development (R&D) Engineer/Scientist here at Honeywell, you will be responsible for developing the next generation of turbine engine-based propulsion systems for the aerospace industry. By collaborating with customers and Honeywell engineering teams, you will define products and supporting technology development roadmaps to meet customer requirements. You will be a technical focal point across Honeywell Aerospace Technologies engineering teams creating solutions and systems that meet the evolving needs of our customers. You will provide Program Management and Project Engineering leadership to execute internal and external programs that define the propulsion and power systems needed by our customers. You will utilize your engineering background and systems engineering fundamentals to manage integrated product teams. You will leverage advanced tools and techniques to deliver high impact business results while serving as a technical leader and coach to other engineers and technology stakeholders.

Responsibilities

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Manage and/or support 1-3 programs simultaneously with >$1M total annual expenditures
Execute advanced engine conceptual design collaborative work preceding and/or resulting from customer pursuits.
Establish program technical requirements, statement of work, sales contracts, program charter, and work breakdown structure
Setup and initiate programs and institute Program Management Operating Systems to ensure contract execution within cost and schedule constraints while achieving technical objectives
Lead supporting project engineering and functional design teams
Utilize the Honeywell Earned Value Management System (EVMS) and subject matter expertise to assess project performance, identify risks early, and implement effective corrective actions 
Lead and develop turbine propulsion system technologies (Combustor, Compressor and Turbine) with analysis, design and test of complex components and systems. 
Effectively manage intellectual property through the timely submittal of patent disclosures

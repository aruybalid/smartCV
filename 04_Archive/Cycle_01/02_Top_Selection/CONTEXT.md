# 02_Top_Selection - Top Job Selection Module

## Purpose
This module guides the final selection of the top 3 job opportunities from the scored list for tailored application development.

## Process

### Input
- `All_Jobs_Scoring_Report.md` from `../01_Job_Scoring/`
- Individual score files for deeper review
- Personal career goals and preferences

### Output
- `Top_3_Jobs.md` - Documented selection with decision matrix and rationale

## Selection Guidelines

### Primary Criteria
1. **Overall Match Score**: Prioritize jobs scoring 75+ unless strategic reasons exist
2. **Gap Analysis**: Prefer roles where gaps are addressable (learning opportunities)
3. **Career Trajectory**: Align with 2-5 year professional goals
4. **Application Feasibility**: Consider time investment vs. probability of success

### Decision Factors to Document
- Why this role over others with similar scores
- Specific strengths that make you a strong candidate
- How you plan to address identified gaps
- Personal interest and motivation level

## Instructions
1. Review the ranked scoring report thoroughly
2. Shortlist 5-7 top job descriptions for deeper consideration
3. Apply the decision matrix in `Top_3_Jobs.md`
4. Provide clear, honest justification for each final selection
5. Note any jobs that were close but ultimately excluded

## Quality Standards
- Selections must be defensible with data from scoring reports
- Avoid selecting solely on highest score if other factors are weak
- Balance ambition with realistic application success probability
- Document reasoning to improve future selection accuracy

## Next Steps After Selection
- Move selected job descriptions to `../04_Archive/`
- Create tailored application folders in `../03_Tailored_Applications/`
- Begin customization using insights from individual score reports

Job Description:

Insight Global is seeking a Mechanical Engineer for a leading engineering-focused client. This individual will be responsible for performing advanced stress and fatigue analyses on critical mechanical systems, ensuring compliance with ASME Section III standards. The role is highly analytical and requires expertise in evaluating system integrity using both ANSYS and hand calculations. The engineer will support a variety of real-time engineering challenges, including emergent issues and long-term system improvements, while also reviewing the work of junior engineers. This is a fast-paced, project-driven environment where priorities shift frequently, making it ideal for candidates who thrive in problem-solving and dynamic technical work.

Day-to-Day:

Perform and review ASME Section III calculations
Conduct stress, fatigue, thermal, seismic, and accident scenario analyses
Analyze components including piping, vessels, supports, and structural systems
Utilize ANSYS and hand calculations to assess system integrity
Review and validate calculations completed by junior engineers
Support customer-driven engineering requests, including urgent issue resolution
Provide both short-term fixes and long-term engineering solutions
Work across dynamic, project-based engineering challenges that shift regularly

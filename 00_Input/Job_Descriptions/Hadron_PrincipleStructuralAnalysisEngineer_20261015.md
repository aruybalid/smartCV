Principal Structural Analysis Engineer – Module Design (P.E. Required) – Role Description

This is a full-time role for a Principal Structural Analysis Engineer – Module Design (P.E. Required) with remote flexibility. The position is ideal for highly experienced structural engineers with deep expertise in nuclear, pressure vessel, and safety-related structural analysis who are passionate about developing the next generation of advanced nuclear energy systems.



The Principal Structural Analysis Engineer will serve as the technical lead for structural load definition, stress analysis, and qualification of Hadron Energy's reactor module and associated safety-related structures. This individual will be responsible for establishing design loads, defining structural requirements, and performing detailed analyses to support reactor licensing, fabrication, and deployment activities.



You will work closely with reactor design, component design, thermal-hydraulics, safety analysis, and licensing teams to ensure all reactor module structures meet applicable ASME Section III, NRC, and industry design requirements. This role will play a critical part in defining the structural basis of the Halo Micro Modular Reactor and supporting its path toward commercial deployment.



Responsibilities

Serve as the technical lead for structural analysis and load definition of the reactor module and associated safety-related systems
Develop and approve structural loading specifications, including thermal, pressure, seismic, transportation, handling, and accident loads
Perform and oversee advanced finite element analysis (FEA) of reactor vessels, containment structures, support systems, and mechanical components
Evaluate structural performance under normal operating conditions, design basis events, and beyond-design-basis scenarios
Develop stress reports and structural qualification documentation in accordance with ASME Section III requirements
Provide engineering direction for component designers and structural analysts throughout the design process
Collaborate with thermal-hydraulic, materials, mechanical, and safety engineers to ensure structural requirements are integrated across all reactor systems
Support NRC licensing activities through preparation of technical reports, regulatory responses, and safety analysis documentation
Review supplier designs, fabrication packages, and engineering analyses to ensure compliance with design requirements
Support testing, validation, and qualification programs for critical reactor structures and components
Establish structural design methodologies, analysis procedures, and engineering standards across the organization
Mentor and provide technical leadership to junior engineers and analysts


Qualifications & Skillsets

Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD in Mechanical Engineering, Structural Engineering, Civil Engineering, Nuclear Engineering, or a related field
Professional Engineer (P.E.) license required
10+ years of experience in structural analysis, pressure vessel design, or nuclear component qualification
Extensive experience with ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code Section III
Advanced proficiency in ANSYS, Abaqus, COMSOL, or equivalent finite element analysis software
Strong understanding of stress analysis, fracture mechanics, fatigue, buckling, thermal stresses, and dynamic loading
Experience developing structural loading specifications and qualification methodologies
Familiarity with seismic analysis, transportation analysis, and accident condition evaluations
Experience supporting NRC licensing, design certification, or safety-related engineering programs is highly desirable
Strong understanding of materials behavior under thermal, pressure, and radiation environments
Excellent analytical, technical writing, and communication skills
Proven ability to lead multidisciplinary engineering efforts and mentor technical teams
Passion for clean energy, engineering excellence, and developing safe, scalable nuclear technologies

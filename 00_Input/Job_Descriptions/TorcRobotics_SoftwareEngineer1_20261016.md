Meet the Team 

Torc is developing Level 4 autonomous semi-truck software to transform how freight moves across the world. As a Software Engineer I on the Metrics for Release (MFR) team, you will contribute to the cloud-based systems and data pipelines that evaluate autonomy performance and support release readiness across Torc Drive. 

This team owns the safety metrics, KPIs, and automated evaluation infrastructure used to validate autonomy behavior and ensure software releases meet safety and performance expectations before deployment. In this role, you will help build and maintain services and workflows that process telemetry and validation data, transforming raw vehicle data into meaningful system signals used to evaluate release quality and system readiness. 

You will work closely with senior engineers and cross-functional teams to support metrics implementation, validation workflows, and scalable cloud-based infrastructure supporting Torc’s autonomous driving platform.

What You’ll Do 

Contribute to the development and maintenance of Python-based services and AWS data pipelines supporting metrics and validation workflows  
Assist in processing and analyzing vehicle telemetry and validation data to generate meaningful safety metrics  
Support implementation of release validation logic tied to automated testing and evaluation pipelines  
Collaborate with systems, validation, and autonomy teams to support implementation of safety and performance metrics  
Investigate pipeline failures, telemetry inconsistencies, and data quality issues using logs and large-scale datasets  
Support workflow orchestration and automation systems used for validation and release readiness  
Participate in code reviews and collaborate with team members to deliver reliable, maintainable software solutions  
Contribute to continuous improvement of engineering practices, tooling, and validation infrastructure

Familiarity with cloud infrastructure and AWS services such as S3, Lambda, ECS, or Step Functions

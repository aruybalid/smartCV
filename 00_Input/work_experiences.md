Info on PhD project experience:
Title of PhD project: “Identification of multi-layer interface systems: a full-field, micro-mechanical approach.” 

Development of an experimental-numerical framework for the full-field identification of constitutive model parameters, focusing on adhesion properties within microelectronic material systems. The research involved the development of a micro-mechanical instrument, including closed-loop feedback control algorithms, for in-situ microscopy testing. Finite element simulations, cohesive zone modeling of interfaces (mixed-mode), microscopic imaging, image processing, digital image correlation, and inverse optimization techniques were used to realize the goal of characterizing the adhesion properties in a flexible light-emitting diode (OLED). Five peer-reviewed research papers have been published in international journals (see below).
